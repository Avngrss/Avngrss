## AI-Assisted Testing

I design controlled workflows in which AI agents support testing without silently changing requirements or weakening checks.

My work in this area includes:

- plan-first test development;
- structured analysis of requirements and product obligations;
- AI-assisted test planning and Playwright test generation;
- explicit responsibilities for planning, generation, execution, and healing;
- project-specific instructions, skills, and architecture rules;
- validation of generated and modified tests;
- evidence requirements and automated quality gates;
- traceability between requirements, tests, executions, and defects;
- controlled healing that preserves the original test obligation;
- Stop conditions when available evidence is insufficient;
- human approval for changes to requirements, scope, or expected behavior.

The objective is not autonomous test generation at any cost. The objective is reliable automation in which AI actions remain constrained, reviewable, and supported by evidence.

## Current Work

### Beyond Playwright Agents

I am writing **Beyond Playwright Agents**, a technical book about the architecture of controlled AI-assisted testing systems built around Playwright agents.

The book explains how to move beyond isolated test generation and build a complete workflow around planning, implementation, execution, evidence, review, and maintenance.

Its central idea is that Playwright agents provide execution capabilities, but a reliable testing system also requires explicit obligations, project context, authority boundaries, quality gates, Stop behavior, and human approval at the points where meaning can change.

The book covers:

- planning as a reviewed contract;
- responsibilities of Planner, Generator, and Healer;
- product obligations as the basis for test implementation;
- project knowledge agents must use instead of guessing;
- evidence strength and traceability;
- validation of generated tests;
- safe maintenance and controlled healing;
- fail-closed behavior when a safe conclusion cannot be supported;
- human-in-the-loop decisions for changes affecting requirements, scope, evidence, or expected behavior.

The core principle is that a passing test is valuable only when it correctly verifies an approved product obligation.

### Playwright AI Workflow Kit

I am developing a reusable **Playwright AI Workflow Kit** that implements the architectural principles described in the book.

The kit provides a controlled workflow for:

1. analyzing approved feature inputs;
2. preparing a reviewable test plan;
3. generating Playwright tests from that plan;
4. validating implementation quality and architecture;
5. executing tests and collecting evidence;
6. investigating failures;
7. proposing safe maintenance changes;
8. stopping when the available evidence does not justify a change.

The kit combines Playwright agents, reusable skills, project rules, quality gates, CI/CD checks, reporting, and traceability. It serves as a practical implementation of controlled AI-assisted automation rather than a replacement for project-specific engineering decisions.
